# 🍯 HoneyChain (.github)

### Smart Beekeeping. Trusted Honey.

Honey Chain is an **AI + IoT + Blockchain platform** connecting the journey of honey from **hive to consumer**.

We combine **smart hive monitoring, AI-powered insights, blockchain-backed traceability, and QR-based verification** to build a more transparent and trusted honey ecosystem.

> **From Hive to Consumer — every batch monitored, verified and traceable.**

## 🐝 What We Do

**🌡️ Smart Hive Monitoring**  
Monitor temperature, humidity, weight and acoustic activity using low-cost IoT devices.

**🧠 AI Hive Intelligence**  
Turn hive data into health insights, anomaly detection, risk alerts and productivity intelligence.

**🍯 Digital Honey Passport**  
Give every harvest and batch a digital identity connecting its journey from hive to market.

**⛓️ Blockchain Traceability**  
Create tamper-evident records for critical honey supply-chain events on Ethereum Sepolia.

**📱 QR Verification**  
Let consumers scan a package and verify its origin, quality and traceability.
