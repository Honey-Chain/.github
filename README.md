# HoneyChain (.github)

### Smart Beekeeping. Trusted Honey.

HoneyChain is an IoT, Machine Learning, and Blockchain platform connecting the lifecycle of honey from hive to consumer.

The platform integrates smart hive environmental monitoring, ML-driven health diagnostics, Ethereum Sepolia smart contracts for tamper-evident provenance, and cryptographic QR verification.

> From Hive to Consumer: every batch monitored, certified, and immutable.

## Platform Capabilities

**Smart Hive Telemetry**  
Real-time environmental monitoring across temperature, humidity, acoustics, and weight with automated drift analysis.

**Machine Learning Diagnostics**  
Independent inference services for colony health classification, disease risk assessment, and yield forecasting.

**Digital Honey Passport**  
Cryptographic batch anchoring on Ethereum Sepolia recording producer metadata, lab purity assays, and custody handoffs.

**Consumer QR Verification**  
Publicly accessible verification interface displaying complete provenance without requiring account registration.
